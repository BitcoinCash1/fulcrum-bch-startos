# Fulcrum BCH — Electrum SPV Server for Bitcoin Cash on StartOS

A fast and efficient Electrum server that connects to your Bitcoin Cash node.

Fulcrum is a high-performance Electrum server that indexes the Bitcoin Cash blockchain from your own full node. It lets you point Electron Cash and other Electrum-protocol wallets at hardware you control, so your addresses are never disclosed to a third-party server, and it is the address and transaction backend BCH Explorer reads from. Bitcoin Cash Node, Bitcoin Cash Daemon, Flowee the Hub and Knuth are all supported as the node it indexes from.
